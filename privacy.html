Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Overview

Signalence is a business intelligence service being developed to help organizations understand information relevant to their reputation, customers, products, services, and brand.

This Privacy Policy describes the general principles governing how Signalence collects, uses, and handles information.

Information we may collect

Signalence may process information obtained from online sources, third-party services, and other permitted sources through applicable access methods, which may include platform-provided application programming interfaces (APIs).

Depending on the source, information may include posts, comments, discussion content, timestamps, source references, and other information made available through that source.

Signalence may also collect information voluntarily provided when someone contacts us, such as a name, email address, company name, and the contents of the inquiry.

How information is used

Information processed by Signalence may be used to identify and analyze business-level reputation, customer experience, and market signals, and to operate, maintain, protect, and improve the service.

Signalence is not designed to identify anonymous individuals, create behavioral profiles of individual users, or sell personal information.

Third-party platforms

Signalence may obtain information from third-party platforms and online services when Signalence has permission or another permitted basis to access that information.

Access to and use of information from third-party services is subject to applicable terms, developer requirements, permissions, technical restrictions, and other requirements associated with those services.

Availability of particular sources within Signalence may depend on authorization, technical availability, contractual requirements, and other factors, and may change over time.

Data minimization

Signalence seeks to limit the collection and retention of information to what is reasonably appropriate for its business intelligence functionality, operation of the service, and applicable requirements.

Data retention

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, its source, the purposes for which it is processed, operational requirements, and applicable legal or third-party requirements.

Signalence may delete, update, refresh, or stop retaining information when it is no longer reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, or when required by applicable law, platform requirements, contractual obligations, or other applicable requirements.

Data sharing

Signalence does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the technical operation of Signalence, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable requirements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, integrity, or operation of Signalence, its users, or others.

Security

Signalence intends to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information being processed.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Signalence develops, changes its services, data practices, technologies, or applicable requirements.

Material revisions will be reflected by updating the effective date shown on this page.

Privacy inquiries

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Signalence's data practices may be submitted through the contact information provided in connection with the Signalence service.
