How Signalence works

Finding the guest signals that deserve attention.

Signalence helps hospitality businesses make sense of guest conversations and reputation signals by identifying what matters and reducing noise.

The problem

Important guest signals are easy to miss.

Hospitality businesses receive signals from many conversations, reviews, and other forms of guest feedback. Most are routine. Some reveal changing expectations, recurring concerns, emerging praise, competitive pressure, or opportunities that deserve attention.

Signalence helps reduce that complexity by identifying meaningful reputation signals and distinguishing them from everyday conversation.
