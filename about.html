How Signalence works

Finding the conversations that deserve attention.

Signalence is being built to help businesses understand meaningful signals from online conversations and other relevant information without requiring teams to manually review large volumes of material.

The problem

Important signals are easy to miss.

Customers increasingly discuss businesses, products, experiences, and brands across public online communities. Those conversations can contain useful information, but the volume makes consistent monitoring difficult.

Signalence is designed to reduce that complexity by finding information relevant to a business and helping distinguish meaningful reputation signals from general conversation.

The Signalence approach

From information to useful insight.

01

Monitor

Identify relevant conversations and other information available through permitted sources and access methods.

02

Understand

Evaluate information for relevance and context to identify potentially meaningful business signals.

03

Surface

Present useful business-level intelligence that helps organizations understand what may deserve attention.

Data use

Focused on business intelligence.

Signalence is being developed to help organizations analyze information relevant to business reputation, customer experience, and market intelligence.

The service is not intended to identify anonymous users, construct personal profiles, or create databases of individuals.

Access to information from third-party platforms is subject to applicable authorization, terms, policies, permissions, and technical requirements.
