How Signalence works

Finding the conversations that deserve attention.

Signalence helps businesses make sense of public conversations by identifying relevant signals and reducing noise.

The problem

Important signals are easy to miss.

Public conversations can contain information that matters to businesses, but the volume of conversation can make important signals difficult to identify.

Signalence helps reduce that complexity by identifying relevant signals and distinguishing them from general conversation.
