How Signalence works

Finding the signals that deserve attention.

Signalence helps businesses make sense of conversations and reputation signals by identifying what matters and filtering out what doesn't.

The problem

Important signals are easy to miss.

Businesses receive signals from conversations, reviews, and other forms of customer feedback. Most are routine. Some reveal changing expectations, recurring concerns, emerging praise, competitive pressure, or opportunities that deserve attention.

Signalence watches for those meaningful signals so your team doesn't have to monitor everything themselves.
